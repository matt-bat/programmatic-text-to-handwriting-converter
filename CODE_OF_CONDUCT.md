# Code of conduct

Scribble Lab welcomes respectful participation from people of every background and experience level.

## Expected behavior

- Be considerate, constructive, and specific.
- Critique ideas and code without attacking people.
- Respect privacy and never post another person's private documents or handwriting.
- Accept maintainer decisions about safety, scope, and project direction.
- Help keep discussions accessible and useful.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, threats, discrimination, or personal attacks
- Publishing private information without permission
- Sexualized or deliberately disruptive conduct
- Requests or contributions intended for impersonation, signature reproduction, fraud, or deceptive documents
- Repeated attempts to bypass the project's identity-free contribution boundary

## Enforcement

Matthew Bateman is responsible for interpreting and enforcing this code. Reports involving sensitive information should use GitHub's private vulnerability reporting feature. Other conduct concerns may be raised through a repository issue that does not expose private information.

Project maintainers may edit or remove content and may temporarily or permanently restrict participation when conduct is harmful to the project or its community.
